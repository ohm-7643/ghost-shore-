              EPISTEMIC SAFEGUARD

              Map Annotations ≠ Modern Ground Truth: All 1854 candidate boxes (such as GS-1854-001, GS-1854-002, GS-1854-003) exist strictly in historical scan pixel coordinates (xyxy) and remain unverified. The public reference sites below (such as Lalbagh Botanical Garden and Ulsoor Lake promenade) are documented civic spaces suitable for observing urban landscape morphology; visiting a public site does not prove an 1854 candidate was at any specific modern spot or has disappeared.

              ⚠️

              Mandatory Field Safety Protocols

              Explore responsibly. Your safety and community respect come first.

              ☀️
              Daylight visits only Observe during daylight hours with clear visibility. Avoid unlit or isolated paths at dusk.
              🚶
              Public pathways only Stay on municipal footpaths, public park trails (e.g. Lalbagh, Ulsoor promenade), or civic roads. Zero trespassing on private property or fenced compounds.
              🚫
              Never enter drains or canals Keep a safe distance from rajakaluves (stormwater channels), deep trenches, and steep bund drops. Never climb slippery stone walls.
              🦺
              Stay clear of construction Active infrastructure and excavation sites are strictly off-limits. Observe only from behind official public safety barriers.
              🤝
              Civic respect & leave no trace Respect local residents and park visitors. Do not dig, remove artifacts, or disturb flora, fauna, or heritage stones.
